GridSense AI · Delhi load study · Jan–Dec 2023, hourly

What pushes Delhi's power demand up, and where it will be needed next

Analysis of 8,737 hourly load readings with weather, holidays, festivals and development zone. A gradient-boosted model learns the load from those inputs, then every "why" below is answered by switching one factor back to normal and measuring how much the predicted load moves.

    Daily pattern

    Two sharp peaks every day, at 15:00 and 23:00

    Average load for each hour, split by development zone. Load roughly doubles in the 15:00 and 23:00 hours and falls back straight after. Real Delhi demand rises and falls gradually through the afternoon and evening, so these one-hour spikes are a quirk of how the dataset was generated. Across the day, High-development zones sit about above Low zones.

    Festivals & holidays

    A festival adds about 500 MW, but a second holiday on the same day adds almost nothing

    "Raw" compares the average load on those days with all other days. "All else equal" holds weather, hour and zone fixed, so it isolates the day itself. When a festival falls on a weekend, as Diwali did on Sunday 12 November, the extra load does not stack. The weekend and the festival each give about the same lift on their own.
